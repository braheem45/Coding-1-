#1  My game is a basketball game. The game will be called basketball brothers. The genre of the game is sports, the objective is to win, score, while also prevent the other player from scoring as much as possible. Its a 5 minute game and the game is first to 11 points counting by ones and twos.

#2 In the game you will be able to shoot, jump, rebound, and block shots. In order to shoot you must press and hold "A", in oder to jump you must press the space bar, in oder to grab a rebound you must press the space bar and while in the air press 'F', and in ordere to block a shot you must press"B". Those are all the4 controls of the game.

#3 2 objects that will definitly be needed in this game are 1 a basketball, and 2 a basketball hoop. The properties of the basketball are round with black lines running across the ball and the ball will be able to bounce around the rim off the backboard and in some other ways. The basketball main purpose will be for shooting, but it can also be blocked, grabbed out the air, and rebounded. The properties of the basketball hoop are tall, square backboard that is clear and has a tiny white square right above the rim. The hoop in the game is where you will score your points, grab most of your rebounds, and potentially block shots as well.
